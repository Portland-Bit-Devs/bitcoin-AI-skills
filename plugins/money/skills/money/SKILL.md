# Money

> **Status: stub.** Structure is in place; the reference material is not written yet.

Money is not a thing so much as a role: whatever good a society converges on to
carry value across time and space. This skill is the vocabulary and the evaluation
framework for reasoning about that role precisely.

## Scope

- **Definition** — money as the most salable good, and what "salable" means across
  scales, space, and time
- **The three functions** — medium of exchange, store of value, unit of account, and
  the order in which a good typically acquires them
- **The monetary properties** — durability, portability, divisibility, fungibility,
  verifiability, and scarcity; how to score a good against each
- **Failure modes** — debasement, inflation, supply expansion, confiscation, capital controls
- **Applying the framework** — gold, fiat currency, bitcoin, and other candidates

## How to use this skill

When a question is about whether something is or isn't money, resist a yes/no answer.
Name which of the three functions it serves, score it on the monetary properties, and
say what would have to change for the verdict to change.

## Stance

This skill supplies a framework, not a verdict. Present the analysis and the tradeoffs;
distinguish clearly between what is definitional, what is empirical, and what is contested.
Do not give investment advice, price predictions, or recommendations to buy or sell —
if asked, say plainly that's outside what this covers.
